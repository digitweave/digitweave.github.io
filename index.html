Help & About

Goal: cross all digits by pairing visible numbers that are equal or sum to 10.

  • Visibility is in right/down directions and can pass over crossed cells.
  • End of row continues into start of next row (same as CLI rules).
  • Tap a digit at the top or bottom edge of a long board to jump to its offscreen cell.
  • For expert play, uncheck Validate selected pair to choose pairs freely. The result shows your pair accuracy.
  • Hint highlights one digit with a match. Use Show Hint button in Controls to hide or show it.
  • Feedback before append blocks appending while matching pairs remain.
  • Auto mode supports strategies: search, max-future, first.
  • Use Collapse empty rows in Controls to compress fully cleared row runs during long games.

This PWA is fully static and stores progress in your browser only.
